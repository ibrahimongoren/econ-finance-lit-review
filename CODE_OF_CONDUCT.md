# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

This project serves researchers across countries, disciplines and career stages. Contributors and
maintainers commit to making participation a respectful and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Engage with arguments and evidence rather than with persons; critique of methods, sources and
  code is welcome when it is specific and constructive.
- Credit the work of others and cite sources accurately: the standard the project applies to
  references applies to discussion as well.
- Accept feedback and decisions graciously, and acknowledge mistakes openly.
- Respect the terms of use of data providers and publishers; do not post licensed full texts,
  credentials or personal data in issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks or personal attacks.
- Publishing the private information of others without their explicit permission.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of sources, data or results.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation in the
project. Concerns can be raised privately with the maintainer through the contact details on the
maintainer's GitHub profile, or reported to GitHub with the **Report content** function.
